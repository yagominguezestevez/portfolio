# Strategic Planning & Development – BizBoost Media

## Overview

Strategic planning project focused on the development of BizBoost Media, a digital marketing agency specialized in helping small businesses improve their online visibility through SEO, social media management and content creation services.

## Objectives

- Analyze the company's external and internal environment.
- Identify competitive advantages and strategic challenges.
- Define a sustainable competitive strategy.
- Design growth opportunities for the next five years.
- Develop key performance indicators (KPIs) for strategic control.

## Strategic Analysis

### External Analysis

- PESTEL Analysis
- Abell's Model
- Porter's Five Forces

### Internal Analysis

- Competitive Advantages
- Resources and Capabilities
- Customer Segmentation

### SWOT Analysis

Assessment of:
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Threats

## Competitive Strategy

Application of Bowman's Strategic Clock to determine the company's market positioning.

**Selected Strategy:** Hybrid Strategy

- Affordable pricing
- High perceived value
- Strong client relationships
- Focus on measurable business results

## Strategic Development Plan

### Horizon 1 (0–12 months)

- Market penetration
- Customer referral programs
- Local SEO initiatives

### Horizon 2 (1–3 years)

- Analytics & Reporting Dashboard Service
- Strategic alliances for market expansion

### Horizon 3 (3–5 years)

- Acquisition of a marketing agency or SEO software startup
- Capability expansion through inorganic growth

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Customer Churn Rate
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Renewal Rate
- Referral Conversion Rate
- Customer Satisfaction Index
- Operating Profit Margin
- Sales Growth
- Return on Investment (ROI)
