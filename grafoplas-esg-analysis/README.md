# ESG & Sustainability Analysis for Grafoplás

## Overview

Environmental sustainability assessment of Grafoplás del Noroeste S.A. based on GRI reporting standards. The project evaluated the company's environmental performance across key areas including energy consumption, greenhouse gas emissions, water usage, waste management and sustainability governance.

## Objectives

- Analyze environmental performance using GRI indicators.
- Evaluate energy consumption and carbon footprint.
- Assess waste management and resource efficiency practices.
- Identify sustainability improvement opportunities.
- Develop recommendations for reducing environmental impact. 

## Key Findings

- Total energy consumption exceeded 1 million kWh during 2024.
- Only 15.6% of electricity consumption originated from renewable sources.
- Calculated indirect carbon emissions of approximately 251 tCO₂.
- Strong environmental certifications and responsible sourcing practices were identified.
- Significant opportunities exist to improve renewable energy adoption and environmental reporting transparency.
